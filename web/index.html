Members are indices into the atlas family, from 0. The
          first member of a class is its representative. The page reads these rows
          from the file after the verifier accepts its exact bytes. n is
          the vertex count, AAG the Avella-Alaminos-Geiss function, and
          dim HH lists dim HH^0 upward.
